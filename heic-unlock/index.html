HEIC Unlock — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

HEIC Unlock does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

What the app does with your files

HEIC Unlock converts .HEIC/.HEIF image files that you select or drag into the app to .JPG or .PNG. All processing happens locally on your PC. Your photos are never uploaded, transmitted over a network, or sent to any server — the app makes no network calls at all.

Data collection

HEIC Unlock does not collect any data about you or your device. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK, and no account or sign-in of any kind.

Data storage

The only files HEIC Unlock creates are the converted JPG/PNG images you explicitly ask it to create, saved either next to your original files or in a folder you choose. Nothing is stored anywhere else, on your device or off it.

Third parties

HEIC Unlock does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example if a future version adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
