Studio Analytics — Terms

Terms of Service

Effective 21 August 2026

Studio Analytics is a private tool built and operated by a single person to track the performance of two of their own TikTok accounts, @UncoveredEmpires and @SiliconHours, alongside their YouTube and Instagram statistics. It connects to TikTok through the official Login Kit, in Sandbox mode, restricted to those two accounts.

This is not a public service. There is no sign-up, no other account can connect to it, and it is never distributed to anyone outside its operator.

What the tool does

  • Reads follower count, likes count, video count and public video metrics for the two authorized accounts.
  • Stores those numbers over time in a local database, to chart growth.
  • Never posts, edits, deletes, or otherwise acts on either account — read access only.

What it doesn't do

  • It does not access any account other than the two listed above.
  • It does not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party.
  • It does not run on any server reachable from outside the operator's own computer.

Ending access

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's own Settings → Security → Manage apps screen, on either account. Revoking access stops all reads immediately; nothing further is required here.
